# Xiaohongshu reference adaptation

The supplied `chat_list.jpg`, `chat_page.jpg`, `chatbot.jpg`, `posts_page.jpg`,
and `profile.jpg` were used as interaction and hierarchy references. They are
not bundled into the app.

FoodMind keeps its paper, forest-green, lime, coral, and muted-neutral palette.
The Android implementation borrows these structural ideas:

- a fixed, labeled five-item bottom navigation on root destinations;
- an edge-to-edge, two-column discovery feed with short metadata and immediate save actions;
- compact white top bars with one or two high-value actions;
- a dark, information-rich profile hero followed by task-oriented cards;
- separate conversation list and conversation destinations;
- rounded incoming/outgoing chat bubbles, attached-source chips, and an input dock that follows the IME;
- high information density without adding unsupported social, follower, commerce, or public-feed claims.

FoodMind deliberately does not copy Xiaohongshu branding, assets, red primary
navigation, follower mechanics, stickers, or commerce behavior.
